Which countries do you ship from?

We ship from China, Germany and the USA to Ghana.

Do you ship cars?

Yes. We ship cars from the USA, Germany and China to Ghana. Send us the car details or auction link and we'll give you a full quote, including shipping and clearing guidance.

Why is the price on the website different from my quote?

The prices on the website are estimates. Shipping costs change often with fuel prices, shipping line charges, exchange rates and world events. Your personal quote shows the real, current price for your shipment.

Can you buy goods for me in China?

Yes. Send us a photo, link or description of what you want. We find the supplier, buy it for you and ship it to Ghana.

I already bought my goods online. Can you ship them?

Yes. Contact us for our warehouse address, have your seller deliver there, and send us the details. We'll handle the rest.

How long does shipping take?

Air cargo usually takes 1–2 weeks and sea freight 5–8 weeks, depending on the route. See the rates section above.

How do I pay?

Contact us on WhatsApp for our current payment options and terms.

Do you help with customs in Ghana?

Yes, we guide you through customs clearance. Import duties and taxes set by the Ghana Revenue Authority are paid by the customer, unless your quote says otherwise.

Are there items you can't ship?

Yes. We can't ship weapons, illegal drugs, counterfeit goods, flammable or hazardous materials, or anything banned in the origin country or Ghana. Ask us if you're unsure.
