1. Quotes and payment

Quotes are valid for 7 days, because supplier prices and exchange rates change. Orders start after we receive the agreed deposit. The balance must be paid before goods are released for delivery.

2. Sourcing and inspection

We inspect goods for quantity, visible damage and match with the agreed specification, and share photos or videos before shipping. We can't guarantee the internal performance of every item, but we will help resolve problems with the supplier.

3. Shipping times

Delivery times are estimates. Delays caused by carriers, weather, port congestion, public holidays (such as Chinese New Year or Christmas) or customs are outside our control.

4. Customs and duties

Import duties and taxes set by the Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) are paid by the customer unless the quote says otherwise. The customer is responsible for making sure the goods are allowed into Ghana.

5. Damage and loss

Please report damage or missing items within 48 hours of receiving your goods, with photos. Optional shipping insurance is available on request.

6. Cancellations and refunds

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund before we pay the supplier. After that, refunds depend on the supplier's policy, minus any costs already incurred.

7. Prohibited items

  • Weapons, ammunition and explosives
  • Illegal drugs
  • Counterfeit or fake branded goods
  • Flammable, toxic or hazardous materials
  • Any goods banned in the origin country (China, Germany or the USA) or Ghana

8. Privacy

We only use your name, contact details and address to process your orders and deliveries. We don't sell your information to anyone.
